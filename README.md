
# NEXA ONE — Rethinking the Advertising Mix

Analytics case study developed for Grand Theft Analytica (GTA) 2.0.

## Objective

To analyse the relationship between advertising expenditure and sales
across 200 markets and identify opportunities to improve the advertising mix.

## Analysis

- Pearson correlation analysis
- Simple linear regression
- Multiple linear regression
- Standardized coefficients
- Channel-wise comparison
- Budget allocation scenario analysis
- External market research

## Key Findings

- Total advertising spend has a strong positive association with sales.
- The multichannel model explains more variation in sales than total spend alone.
- TV shows the strongest observed channel-level association.
- Social Media shows a meaningful positive association.
- Print shows weak association with sales.

## Recommendation

Maintain TV as a core channel while testing a controlled shift
from Print toward Social Media.
